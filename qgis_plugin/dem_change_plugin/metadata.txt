description=Probabilistic DEM change detection with spatially variable uncertainty surfaces. Computes a Difference of DEMs (DoD) and per-pixel probability of significant change based on the analytical intersection of two Gaussian error distributions.
version=1.0.0
author=Phillipe Wernette, PhD
email=pwernett@msu.edu
about=This plugin computes statistically significant elevation changes between two DEMs while accounting for spatially variable uncertainty in both epochs. Inputs can be any GDAL-readable raster. Error surfaces may be either spatially variable rasters or a single global scalar value (interpreted as a 95% confidence interval). Outputs are Cloud-Optimized GeoTIFFs (ZSTD compression, predictor 3, BigTIFF, embedded overviews): a raw elevation difference raster and a probability-of-change raster [0,1]. Processing is tile-based (512x512) and parallelised across available CPU cores. Developed at the Michigan State University Remote Sensing & GIS Research and Outreach Services (RS&GIS) centre.
